# About Me

My name is **Danial Kordmodanlou**. I'm a **Software Engineer at [EvenUp](https://www.evenuplaw.com)**, where I build **agentic AI and full-stack systems** that help personal injury law firms work faster and expand access to justice.

Before EvenUp, I was a **Machine Learning Associate at the [Vector Institute](https://vectorinstitute.ai)**, building a real-time VR firefighter training system with LLM-driven coaching and pose-deviation detection.

Prior to them, I completed my **MSc in Computer Science** at York University, Toronto (GPA: 4.0/4.0), researching illusory parallax in stereoscopic VR at the BioMotion Lab. I love programming and learning new things! I work across **Machine Learning & AI** (LLMs, Agentic AI, Computer Vision), **Full Stack Development** (React, TypeScript, FastAPI, cloud deployment), and **Game Development** (Unity3D, multiplayer systems, VR/AR). I'm passionate about computing science and love working on challenging problems and coming up with creative solutions!
